# Diffusion Language Model Daily Recommendation

Last updated: 2026-09-17

## Predict, Don't Iterate: Efficient Adaptive-Length Infilling for Diffusion Language Models

- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02108
- **Domain:** Diffusion language models
- **Authors:** Haobo Xu, Sirui Chen, Yuanchen Bei, Lingjie Chen, Yuchen Yan, Dongqi Fu, Jingrui He, Hanghang Tong
- **Posted:** 2026-09-02
- **Venue signal:** Accepted at EMNLP 2026 Main Conference, according to the arXiv record
- **Code:** https://github.com/Hsu1023/PILL
- **Broader trend:** Turning the structural advantages of bidirectional, any-order generation into practical editing and infilling systems

### Recommendation

This is the diffusion language model paper I would read today. PILL attacks a concrete weakness in masked DLM infilling: the model can use both left and right context, but it must normally know the missing span length before decoding. The paper is unusually well scoped and empirically broad for a new result, evaluating five DLM backbones across eight code and text benchmarks; it reports a +4.8 average code pass-rate gain and +6.0 BLEU-2 over the strongest adaptive-length baseline while running 1.82x faster.

### Why it matters now

The DLM literature is moving from demonstrating parallel generation toward identifying workloads where non-autoregressive structure is genuinely useful. Infilling is one of the clearest such workloads, and PILL removes a practical fixed-canvas constraint without retraining the backbone. Its value is less a claim that DLMs beat autoregressive models generally and more a credible systems recipe for exploiting native bidirectional conditioning.

### Core idea

- Predict the missing span length from a single mask token's bidirectional hidden state using a lightweight probe.
- Expand that estimate to nearby lengths and denoise all candidates together with slot-isolated attention and interpolated position IDs.
- Select the final span in one extra pass using a score that combines internal coherence with compatibility with the observed suffix.

### What to read skeptically

The headline comparison is against fixed-length DLM decoding and adaptive DLM methods (DAEDAL and CAL), not a strong FIM-trained autoregressive infiller, so it does not establish an end-to-end DLM advantage over the best AR alternative. Runtime is measured on one NVIDIA A40 and grows with the number of candidate slots; the appendix also shows that probe coverage is the largest remaining failure source and that candidate selection leaves a sizable gap to the oracle. The paper is too new for meaningful citation evidence.
