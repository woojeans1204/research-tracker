## SDAR: A Synergistic Diffusion-AutoRegression Paradigm for Scalable Sequence Generation

- Paper: https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1110/
- Domain: diffusion language models; blockwise discrete diffusion
- Publication: Findings of ACL 2026, July 2026. The original preprint appeared in October 2025; the conference publication falls within the six-month window.
- Why now: SDAR offers a well-supported way to evaluate the practical middle ground between autoregression and diffusion. Its compute- and scale-matched comparisons, models from 1.7B to 30B, real hardware measurements, and released models and code make it a useful reference as the field moves from theoretical parallelism to deployable systems.

### Core idea

- Adapt a pretrained autoregressive model into a blockwise diffusion model with brief additional training.
- Generate blocks sequentially while using discrete denoising to predict tokens in each block in parallel.
- Tune block size and decoding thresholds to trade generation quality for speed; evaluate scaling and structured scientific tasks.

### Broader trend

Hybrid autoregressive-diffusion architectures use efficient AR pretraining and caching while retaining local bidirectional generation. The emphasis is shifting toward measured quality-latency tradeoffs rather than tokens-per-forward-pass alone.

### Critical notes

The reported up to 2.3x wall-clock speedup on H200 is workload-specific: the paper identifies block size 64 and batch size 4, and notes that the advantage narrows in throughput-oriented regimes. The result is not a blanket speedup over all optimized AR serving or speculative decoding. This is a hybrid, not a demonstration that fully non-autoregressive diffusion wins end to end; inspect conversion cost, benchmark-level changes, and baseline implementation details. The paper identifies reinforcement-learning-based alignment as untested.
